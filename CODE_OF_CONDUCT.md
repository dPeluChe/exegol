# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
([en español](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/es/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)).

In short: be respectful and constructive, in English or in Spanish. Harassment, insults, personal
attacks and publishing someone's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at antonio@dpeluche.dev. Reports are reviewed
promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer applies the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

## Reportar

Reporta cualquier conducta inaceptable al mantenedor en antonio@dpeluche.dev. Los reportes se
revisan pronto y se tratan de forma confidencial.
